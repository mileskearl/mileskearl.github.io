What I'm doing right now

I'm a junior in Orem, Utah, spending this fall interning at a company called After — two sessions a week, learning how a business actually works: what things cost, how they get priced, where the money goes, and how you find someone willing to pay you. I'd rather build something new than sell something that already exists, so I'm using the fall to figure out what that something is. This site is where I'll keep the work as it happens.

This is also the first website I've ever built, which is why it looks the way it looks. I'd rather ship it and fix it than sit on it.
